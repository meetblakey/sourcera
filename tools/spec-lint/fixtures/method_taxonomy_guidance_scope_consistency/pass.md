## 2.1 Overview {#2.1-overview}

Users MAY opt out of Method guidance (instructional copy and recommended prompts), but every Buyer Workspace remains on the same canonical platform phases, roles, and controls; there is no Method-disabled workflow or alternate lifecycle.

Any Workspace surface MUST NOT define alternate phase names, alternate duration totals, or a parallel procurement lifecycle. A user MAY opt out of guidance but MUST NOT bypass Workspace, Use Case, Requirement, phase-advancement, RBAC, audit, retention, or pricing constraints.
